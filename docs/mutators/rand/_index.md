---
title: "rand(args)"
weight: 6
symbol: rand
---

Randomly [fills](rand_times_value/) or [clears](rand_times/) a specified number of times cells in the quadrille.

> **Note**
>
> For deterministic (repeatable) randomness, explicitly call [randomSeed(seed)](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/randomSeed/) before `rand(args)`.
